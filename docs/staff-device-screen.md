# Attendance on a shared phone

M34 remains in progress. The default-off `staffTeam` preview now connects the paired-screen backend to an actual outlet roster and each member’s My shift screen. The existing `kitchenScreen` preview can also expose preparation work to a member with actual order-read permission. The server endpoint starts disabled and remains undeployed. Workflows and public publication stay held.

Each installation supplies its own backend connection and private service configuration. The owner enables order-phone attendance for the member in Team, sets their PIN in Shared screens, and pairs the browser. Setup QR codes carry the installation’s public connection and opted-in preview flags; they carry no device token, PIN, staff session or owner account. A rider appears in PIN settings only after their outlet’s attendance method is enabled. A formerly configured PIN remains clearable after that method is removed. Current owner authorization and method eligibility are checked again by the backend.

The Who’s here roster contains actual assigned humans and their latest recorded attendance, method and recorder. Its fixed server projection excludes contact numbers, Auth identities, PIN verifiers and private receipt identifiers. Pages are bounded at 50 members. Fresh reads check the actual device, PIN session, assignment, method and clock. An owner who has no assignment, attendance profile or attendance at the outlet is not added to the shared roster. Another person’s card signs the current member out before opening that person’s own PIN form. Riders receive attendance access without kitchen orders or money access.

My shift reuses the English/Bangla link presentation while authenticating with the enrolled device and current member’s PIN. Its native journal is separate from the owner’s shop database and from kitchen tickets. A version-3 upgrade preserves the existing screen state and kitchen originals. The complete attendance intent is committed locally before HTTP; lost replies retain its original identifiers, recorder scope and response. Closing an unfinished tap is also saved before HTTP, so offline closure survives a reload and blocks a delayed submission. A later PIN for the same member and device can inspect or close the earlier original but cannot silently retry it. A different member cannot read that journal. Storage failure blocks a new effect while leaving manual copy available.

The board refreshes every five seconds while visible and through Refresh board. Loading further pages pauses automatic replacement until an explicit refresh. Link, manual and device attendance use the same canonical check-ins and member lock. Late, Absent, Swapped and extra-help controls copy notes for the manager; these notes do not invent attendance or grant staff permission to edit another member. The owner/assigned-manager Team screen remains the place to record attendance manually. An unavailable clipboard exposes selectable text and a separate handoff confirmation.
