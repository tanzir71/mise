# M16 · Today and closing

Status: foundations in progress; M16 is not complete. The new Today screen, denomination controls, offline closing flow, share/PDF actions, reference comparisons and final delivery checks remain.

The pure core derives same-currency financial totals from the outlet's civil day, with active sales/payment splits, sale/order counts, credit given and net collections. It keeps physical cash movement separate: retained cash receipts + gross cash khata collections − actual cash refunds. Cancellation and payment/refund correction records retain their existing meaning; refunds are not deducted twice. Waiting orders, completed-order durations, active low stock and due reminder customers come from real records, with no sample metrics.

Closing commands freeze the reviewed summary, reject changes or a crossed local midnight, require a variance note when extra cash is not explicitly recorded as sales, and support an explicit linked cash sale for an overage. Stable request IDs make repeated saves idempotent; one active closing per outlet/currency/day prevents competing saves. Both closing and overage undo share an atomic audit transaction and are available once on the same recorded shop day. Later sales are retained. A saved summary preserves the actor, time, currency, timezone, counted cash, original expectation, variance and optional walk-in receipt. Its shareable text explains that opening float and unrecorded expenses are not included.

Schema 7 adds `dayClosings` and an audit index without changing existing records. Existing sale correction routes protect closing-owned receipts. The new `today` flag defaults off while the current Today view remains available.
