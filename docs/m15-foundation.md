# M15 · Stock foundation

The foundation was published with `stock: false` while the reference-based phone/desktop list, supplier drafts and night-count screens were connected. See [M15 delivery](m15-delivery.md) for the UI and its verification. The roadmap is checked only after the full delivery is verified.

Stock quantities use up to three decimal places in the item's unit. JSON stores readable quantities; calculations use integer thousandths and reject overflow, negative stock and unsupported precision. To order rounds the positive target deficit up to a whole pack. Kilograms/grams, litres/millilitres and pieces/hali/dozen convert exactly; hali means four pieces. A strip's contents are never assumed. Packs use the item's explicitly entered pack size. Changing a stock unit requires a new item so historical movements keep their meaning.

Items start from an explicit physical count, including zero. Their target, category, aliases, supplier and low threshold are editable; the default low threshold is half the target, rounded down to the supported precision. No recipe deduction or fabricated opening stock is introduced. Every received/used/waste/count action appends a movement with before/after, unit/name snapshots, actor, time and audit event. Night counts include zero variances, share one batch ID and commit atomically; skipped items are untouched. Revision checks prevent a stale preview from replacing another tab's changes.

Quick updates are deterministic previews, with 48 English/unit-variant fixtures plus Bengali digits and local-name aliases. The parser accepts quantity-first and item-first phrases, separates multiple clauses and requires editing for ambiguity, incompatible units, duplicate item clauses or insufficient stock. It treats input as data. It never executes instructions or writes inventory; Save must apply a reviewed set through the database command.

Undo appends the opposite movement, once within 24 hours, preserving later usage. A later physical count must be undone first; reversing a receipt cannot make current stock negative. Archival preserves history, allows restoration and frees the active name while preventing a conflicting restore.

Supplier drafts group pack-rounded needs by active supplier, with unassigned stock kept available for manual copying. Text follows the configured Bangla/English languages and splits long lists into complete, reviewable parts. WhatsApp and Copy do not log completion. Explicit Sent records the actual recipient/text/part snapshot and is idempotent under repeated taps. Correcting a sent record retains its history and does not claim to retract an external message. Sending a draft never receives inventory, creates a purchase or changes customer credit.

IndexedDB version 6 adds `stockItems`, `stockMoves`, `suppliers` and `supplierMessages`, plus lookup and uniqueness indexes. Existing catalogue, orders, notes, sales, refunds, customers, khata and audit records are preserved. All writes and their actor/time events share one transaction, including multi-item counts and supplier completion.

Local validation: lint, TypeScript/Astro checks and the portable production build pass; all 279 unit tests and 119 browser tests pass.
