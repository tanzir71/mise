- 2026-11-16 (M14 cancellation and refunds): retain the original receipt, exclude a cancelled order from revenue and record physical refunds separately. Cancel credit against its own unpaid lot first, preserving unrelated debt age; return at most the current balance and record any previously collected excess as money owed back. Refund entries change net collections, never the debt balance. A refund is a manually confirmed transfer, with partial amounts and ten-minute record correction; cancellation cannot be undone while an actual refund remains recorded. Schema 5 only adds the refund table and indexes.
- 2026-11-16 (M14 correction dependency): correcting a khata payment that precedes an active order-credit cancellation requires undoing that cancellation first. Its credit-return/refund calculation used the recorded payment; changing that payment alone could recreate debt for a cancelled order. Reversal pairs remove this dependency once cancellation is undone.
- 2026-11-16 (M16 cash accounting constraint): expected cash uses retained cash receipts (including cancelled orders until refunded), gross active cash khata payments, then subtracts actual active cash refunds. Do not subtract refunds twice by starting from net collections or net revenue. Khata collection totals include archived customers because archival must not erase past money movements.
